# Beef and Broccoli

---

## Step 1 — Beef + Marinade

- [ ] **340 g** skirt steak, sliced ~6 mm thick *against the grain*
- [ ] **5 ml** light soy sauce or shoyu *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **5 ml** sesame oil *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **10 ml** Shaoxing wine *(2 tsp)*
- [ ] **1 g** MSG *(¼ tsp)*
- [ ] **3 g** cornstarch *(1 tsp)*
- [ ] **1.5 g** baking soda *(a bit over ¼ tsp)*
- [ ] **½ tsp** kosher salt *(or ¼ tsp fine table salt)*

---

## Step 2 — Sauce *(mix in a small bowl)*

- [ ] **15 ml** dark soy sauce *(1 tbsp)*
- [ ] **15 ml** light soy sauce *(1 tbsp)*
- [ ] **30 ml** oyster sauce *(2 tbsp)*
- [ ] **20 g** sugar

---

## Step 3 — Cornstarch Slurry *(mix in a separate small bowl)*

- [ ] **6 g** cornstarch *(2 tsp)*
- [ ] **15 ml** water *(1 tbsp)*

---

## Step 4 — Broccoli / Broccolini

- [ ] **340 g** broccoli or broccolini
  - Florets separated
  - Stems sliced on a sharp bias into 2.5 cm pieces (peel if tough)

---

## Step 5 — Stir-Fry

- [ ] **30 ml** peanut, rice bran, or soybean oil *(2 tbsp)*
- [ ] **8 g** minced garlic *(3–4 medium cloves)*
- [ ] **5 g** minced ginger *(about a 1 cm piece)*
- [ ] **15 ml** Shaoxing wine *(1 tbsp)*
- [ ] → add the **Sauce** (Step 2)
- [ ] → add the **Slurry** (Step 3) — stir it first, cornstarch settles

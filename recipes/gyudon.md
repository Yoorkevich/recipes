# Gyudon

**Serves:** 2

*Japanese simmered beef and onion rice bowl, optionally topped with an onsen tamago.*

---

## Rice *(start first)*

- [ ] **320 g** rice
- [ ] **435 ml** water
- [ ] Cook the rice

---

## Onsen Tamago *(optional, takes 30 min)*

- [ ] **2** eggs *(one per bowl)*
- [ ] Heat water in a saucepan to **65 °C** (don't let it boil)
- [ ] Add the eggs and leave for 30 min

---

## Sauce *(in the pan you will cook the beef in)*

- [ ] **140 ml** water
- [ ] **100 ml** mirin
- [ ] **60 ml** soy sauce
- [ ] **25 g** brown sugar *(2 tbsp)*
- [ ] **1 g** dashi powder *(a sprinkle, optional)*

---

## Onion

- [ ] **75 g** onion, thinly sliced *(½ onion)*, add to the sauce

---

## Beef

- [ ] Bring the sauce to a boil over high heat, lid on
- [ ] **200 g** beef loin, thinly sliced, add once boiling and cook a few minutes until cooked through
- [ ] Let the beef stew in the sauce for ~5 min

---

## Serve

- [ ] Spoon the beef, onion and sauce over the rice
- [ ] Top with an onsen tamago
